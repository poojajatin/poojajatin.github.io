Our Story

Our path to marriage was paved by the blessings and wisdom of our families. Pooja and Jatin were first introduced through a mutual family connection, beginning a dialogue rooted in respect, shared values, and compatible aspirations.

What followed was a beautiful period of discovery. Through video calls and occasional family gatherings, we exchanged thoughts on life, career, and future goals, realizing how perfectly our temperaments complement one another—Pooja’s spirited warmth balancing Jatin's grounded perspective.

With the full support and happiness of our elders, our connection deepened from acquaintance to genuine affection, culminating in the joyous decision to unite our lives and families forever.

With the blessings of our families, our journey begins.
