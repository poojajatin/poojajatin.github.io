Our Story

Our journey began not with a spark, but a comfortable warmth. Pooja, with her infectious laugh and boundless energy, first met Jatin, the calm and steady force, through mutual friends at a small gathering in Delhi. What started as casual conversations quickly evolved into late-night talks, sharing dreams, and exploring the city together.

From navigating the bustling streets of Chandni Chowk to quiet evenings discussing classic cinema, we found our lives harmonizing perfectly. Jatin loved how Pooja saw color in everything; Pooja admired Jatin's grounded wisdom. Four years later, under a sky full of stars, Jatin asked the question that set us on our path to forever.

And now, our forever begins.
